# Community Guidelines — WAGMI

**Last updated**: September 28, 2026

*Leggi in [italiano](./community-guidelines).*

WAGMI is a place to train, share progress, and motivate each other. To keep it that way, we enforce a **zero-tolerance** policy toward offensive content and abusive behavior.

WAGMI is for people **18 years of age or older** — the app includes Community social features (finding people who train near you, friends, chat and the option to look for dating and relationships), location sharing, and the ability to chat and meet in person with unknown users.

## What is never allowed

- **Spam** — unsolicited promotional content, chain messages, misleading links
- **Offensive content** — hate speech, discrimination based on race, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability, or any other personal characteristic
- **Harassment** — threats, repeated insults, stalking behavior toward another user
- **Nudity or sexual content** — explicit sexual images, videos, or text
- **Violence** — content that depicts, incites, or threatens real violence
- **False identity** — profiles that impersonate another person or provide deliberately false information
- Any other content that is illegal under applicable law

This applies to everything you post on WAGMI: posts, comments, chat messages, photos, videos, and your profile's name and bio.

## How to report

Every post, comment, message, and profile has a **"Report"** option. Choose the reason that fits best and, if you like, add a note. Reports are anonymous to the person reported. You can also block a user at any time: you will no longer see their content, and they will no longer see yours.

## What happens after a report

- Every report is reviewed by an administrator **within 24 hours**.
- Content reported by multiple different users is automatically hidden pending review.
- If the report is well-founded, the content is removed and the author may be suspended.
- For serious or repeated violations, the account is suspended: while suspended, you cannot post new content, and your existing content is not visible to other users, until an administrator reviews the situation.

## Zero tolerance

We make no exceptions for "good intentions" or content that was "just a joke": if content violates these guidelines, it is removed, regardless of the intent behind it.

## Questions or appeal

If you believe a moderation decision was a mistake, write to **support@wagmifit.it** explaining the situation: we will review it again.
